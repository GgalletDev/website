# Privacy Policy — SmashDice

Last updated: May 25, 2026

Welcome to SmashDice, a game developed by GG Games Labs.

This Privacy Policy explains what data may be collected when you use the game, how it is used, and your rights regarding that data.

---

## 1. Data Collected

SmashDice only collects data necessary for the game to function properly.

### Game Data

The game may store:

- the username chosen by the player;
- the player’s top 10 scores.

This data is stored using Google Firebase Firestore to enable game saves and leaderboard functionality.

### Anonymous Authentication

SmashDice uses Firebase Anonymous Authentication to create an anonymous technical identifier used to save player data.

No email registration or account creation is required.

### Advertisements

The game uses Google AdMob to display:

- interstitial ads;
- rewarded ads.

AdMob may collect certain technical and advertising-related data according to its own privacy policies.

### Advertising Consent

The game uses Google UMP (User Messaging Platform) to manage user consent regarding personalized advertising in compliance with applicable regulations, including GDPR in Europe.

---

## 2. How Data Is Used

Collected data is used only to:

- save player progress and high scores;
- ensure proper game functionality;
- display advertisements;
- improve stability and user experience.

---

## 3. Data Sharing

Data may be processed by the following third-party services:

- Google Firebase
- Google AdMob
- Google UMP

These services may process data according to their own privacy policies.
